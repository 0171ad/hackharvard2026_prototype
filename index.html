    FAQ

    Have more questions? Contact us at team@hackharvard.io

    What is a hackathon?

    A weekend-long building sprint where students team up to make software, hardware, art, tools, and experiments from scratch.

    Who can come?

    HackHarvard is open to undergraduate students. Teams can include students from different schools and experience levels.

    What does it cost?

    The event is free for accepted hackers, including meals, workshops, mentorship, and event programming.

    What if I do not have a team?

    You can form a team at the event. There will be time and space to meet other hackers before building begins.

    When does hacking officially begin?

    Hacking officially begins after the opening ceremony at approximately 4:00 PM on Friday, October 16.

    Do I need to attend the opening ceremony?

    The opening ceremony takes place Friday from 3:00–4:00 PM. We strongly encourage all hackers to attend so they do not miss important event information.

    What if I arrive late on Friday?

    Please contact the HackHarvard team as soon as possible if you expect to miss the 12:00–3:00 PM registration window. Late arrival may affect your ability to participate.

    Are there activities besides hacking?

    Yes. HackHarvard includes talks, workshops, sponsor activities, mentorship, and other programming. The schedule includes a sponsor fair on Saturday morning and a SpaceX AI workshop on Saturday afternoon.

    When are projects due?

    All projects must be submitted on Devpost by 8:00 AM on Sunday, October 18.

    When does judging take place?

    Judging takes place from 9:00 AM–12:00 PM on Sunday, October 18. Judges will complete training and debriefing from 8:00–9:00 AM.

    What happens after I submit my project?

    After submissions close at 8:00 AM, judges evaluate projects from 9:00 AM–12:00 PM. Judges deliberate from 1:00–2:30 PM, and winners are announced during the closing ceremony.

    Do I need to attend on Sunday?

    Yes. Hackers should be present at the venue by 9:00 AM on Sunday for judging. The closing ceremony will take place from 3:00–4:00 PM.

    Is virtual judging available?

    No. Judging for HackHarvard 2026 will take place in person.

    Does the venue stay open overnight?

    No. The venue closes overnight, and overnight stays are not permitted. Participants must arrange their own accommodations and return the following day. Daily re-check-in is not required.

    Where can I stay?

    Participants are responsible for arranging their own accommodations for the duration of HackHarvard. While we are not able to offer housing this year, we encourage attendees to explore nearby hotels, hostels, or short-term rentals.

    How does travel reimbursement and financial aid work?

    Travel reimbursement and financial aid are limited and are not guaranteed. Selected participants will receive separate communication with information about eligibility, amounts, covered expenses, and next steps. Participants should not assume funding will be available unless they receive confirmation.

    Can I apply after the deadline?

    Applications are closed. Late applications and requests to take another participant’s place are not guaranteed and will only be considered if space becomes available.

    Can I appeal an admissions decision?

    Applicants may contact the HackHarvard team with a substantive reason for reconsideration. Appeals are reviewed individually and do not guarantee admission.

    Can I still volunteer as a judge?

    The judge panel is currently full. Mentor opportunities may still be available.

    Can I volunteer as a mentor?

    Yes. Mentors may come and go during Friday and Saturday. The busiest building periods are generally Friday from 4:00–8:00 PM and Saturday from 12:00–8:00 PM.

    Does HackHarvard provide travel or lodging for mentors?

    HackHarvard does not currently provide travel reimbursement or lodging for mentors.
